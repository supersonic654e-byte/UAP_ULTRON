# Market Analysis (Bangladesh healthcare robotics)

> Working note — numbers from our own market research and public WHO/industry
> sources at the time of writing. Re-verify before any external use.

## Problem signals

| Signal | Value |
|---|---|
| Registered hospitals | 2,000+ |
| Health spending | ~USD 32 per capita |
| Nursing shortage | ~82% (need ~310,500, have ~56,700) |
| Bed occupancy | ~80% with ~18.5-day average stay |
| Hospitals with surveillance/operational data | ~0% |
| Imported hospital robots | USD 100k–200k |
| Local medical-robotics companies | 0 |

## The gap

- Imported robots (Xenex, Tru-D, TUG) are expensive, built for high-staff
  developed-country hospitals, and not serviceable locally.
- No local company is building for Bangladesh constraints: crowding, basic
  WiFi, narrow corridors, tight budgets, local spare parts.

## Our positioning

## Roadmap-to-market

- InsightV1.0: environment + logistics + behaviour intelligence (first real
  deployment).
- VitalsV2.0: patient monitoring add-on.
- MedAssistV3.0: AI diagnosis/outbreak prediction.
- Business model: free pilot units → hardware + cloud subscription → regional
  scale.

## Honest caveats

- Unit cost, runtime, and reliability numbers must come from our own bench and
  pilot results — do not quote unverified figures.
- Clinical impact claims are **to be validated in pilot**.
